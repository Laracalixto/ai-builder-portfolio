# AI Prospecting Assistant — Fictional Demo

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates how an AI agent can support B2B prospecting by transforming company information into structured commercial intelligence.

The proposed workflow helps organize business context, identify potential challenges, evaluate alignment with an Ideal Customer Profile (ICP), and suggest personalized outreach.

**Important:** This is an independent demonstration using fictional company data. It does not use CUB's internal systems, customer information, or confidential materials.

## 1. Fictional Company

- **Company:** Horizonte Empreendimentos
- **Industry:** Real estate development
- **Business model:** Residential real estate projects
- **Location:** Campinas, São Paulo
- **Company size:** Medium-sized company (fictional scenario)

## 2. Business Context

Horizonte Empreendimentos manages multiple residential projects and may deal with recurring financial and operational processes, such as:

- Payment collection from customers.
- Monitoring overdue payments.
- Financial reconciliation.
- Communication with customers about outstanding balances.
- Coordination between finance and customer service teams.

These are hypothetical business challenges created for this demonstration, not verified facts about a real company.

## 3. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) Analysis

Based on the fictional scenario, the company may be a potential fit for a solution focused on financial automation and receivables management.

**Potential fit indicators:**

- Multiple residential projects.
- Recurring customer payment processes.
- Need for financial visibility and organization.
- Potential complexity in payment tracking and reconciliation.

**Potential decision-makers:**

- Business owners and partners.
- Financial directors and managers.
- Administrative and financial coordinators.

The agent's role is to organize available information and identify relevant hypotheses. These indicators should be validated through further research and qualification.

## 4. Potential Business Opportunities

Based on the hypothetical context, the agent could identify opportunities such as:

| Potential challenge | Opportunity to explore |
|---|---|
| Manual payment tracking | Automating collection workflows |
| Difficulty monitoring overdue payments | Improving payment visibility |
| Time-consuming reconciliation | Streamlining financial processes |
| Disconnected communication channels | Centralizing customer communication |

These are discovery hypotheses, not confirmed problems.

## 5. Personalized Outreach Example

**Suggested approach:**

"Hi! I noticed that companies managing multiple real estate projects often deal with complex payment and receivables processes.

I'd like to understand how your team currently manages collection, reconciliation, and communication with customers, and whether there are opportunities to make these processes more efficient.

Would you be open to a brief conversation?"

The message is intentionally consultative. It uses the business context as a starting point rather than assuming the company has a specific problem.

## 6. Proposed Agent Workflow

1. Receive the company name and available public information.
2. Organize relevant business data.
3. Identify possible decision-makers.
4. Evaluate potential ICP alignment.
5. Map possible business challenges.
6. Generate personalized outreach suggestions.
7. Present the findings in a structured format for human review.

## 9. Project Scope and Limitations

This is a conceptual demonstration created with fictional data to illustrate how an AI-assisted prospecting workflow can be structured.

The examples and business challenges are hypothetical. The suggested insights and outreach messages require human review and validation before real-world use.

No live integrations, automated external data retrieval, or CRM connections are implemented in this demonstration.
