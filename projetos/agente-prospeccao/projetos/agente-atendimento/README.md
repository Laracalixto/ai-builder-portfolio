# AI Customer Support & Knowledge Agent

## Overview
AI agent designed to make product and process knowledge more accessible, supporting customer service and internal teams in finding information from existing training materials.

## Business Context
Developed during my experience at CUB, where product knowledge was distributed across recorded product demonstration meetings and internal training recordings.

## Objective
Make it easier to consult information from recorded materials and obtain clear answers to questions about products and processes.

## Key Capabilities
- Consult information contained in product demonstration recordings.
- Use internal training recordings as a knowledge source.
- Answer questions based on the available materials.
- Support access to product and process knowledge.

## Tools
- Manus
- Claude

## My Contribution
- Organized and prepared knowledge sources for the agent.
- Configured the agent's instructions and behavior.
- Developed and refined prompts.
- Tested the agent's responses using questions related to the available materials.

## Project Status
This project was originally developed and used in CUB's corporate environment. I no longer have access to the original agent or its internal materials.

This documentation describes my experience and contribution. No confidential company information, internal documents, recordings, or customer data are included.
