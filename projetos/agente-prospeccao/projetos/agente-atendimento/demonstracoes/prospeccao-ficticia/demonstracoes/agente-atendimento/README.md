# AI Customer Support Agent — Fictional Demo

## Project Overview

A practical demonstration of an AI agent designed to answer customer questions using a structured knowledge base.

The goal is to make information easier to access, provide consistent answers, and support customer service teams.

**Important:** This is an independent demonstration using fictional company information. It does not reproduce CUB's internal materials or original agent.

## 1. Fictional Company

**Company:** Conecta Gestão  
**Industry:** Business management software  
**Product:** Financial management platform  
**Target audience:** Small and medium-sized businesses

## 2. Knowledge Base

For this demonstration, the agent has access to the following fictional product information:

| Topic | Information |
|---|---|
| Payment tracking | Customers can view payment status in the financial dashboard. |
| Payment methods | The platform supports bank slips and Pix. |
| Notifications | Payment reminders can be sent by email. |
| Reconciliation | The platform provides tools to help monitor and reconcile payments. |
| Customer support | Users can contact the support team through the help center. |

## 3. Example Customer Questions

### Question 1
**Customer:** How can I check whether a payment has been received?

**AI Agent:** You can check the payment status through the financial dashboard. There, you can view the information available for each transaction.

### Question 2
**Customer:** Which payment methods are supported?

**AI Agent:** According to the available product information, the platform supports bank slips and Pix.

### Question 3
**Customer:** Can I send payment reminders to customers?

**AI Agent:** Yes. The platform supports payment reminders sent by email.

### Question 4
**Customer:** Does the platform integrate with my ERP?

**AI Agent:** I don't have enough information in the current knowledge base to confirm which ERP integrations are available. Please contact the support team through the help center so they can verify your specific case.

## 4. Agent Workflow

1. Receive a customer question.
2. Identify the subject of the request.
3. Consult the available knowledge base.
4. Formulate an answer using the information found.
5. Identify when information is missing.
6. Direct the customer to the appropriate support channel when necessary.

## 5. Expected Benefits

- Faster access to product information.
- More consistent customer responses.
- Reduced time spent searching for internal documentation.
- Better support for customer service teams.
- Clearer identification of questions that require human assistance.

## 6. Skills Demonstrated

- AI agent configuration.
- Prompt engineering.
- Knowledge organization.
- Customer experience.
- Process analysis.
- Response evaluation.
- Responsible use of AI and information validation.
