# AI Prospecting Assistant — Fictional Demo

## Project Overview
A practical demonstration of how an AI agent can support B2B prospecting by organizing company information, identifying potential business needs, evaluating ICP fit, and suggesting personalized outreach.

**Important:** This is an independent demonstration created with fictional company data. It does not use CUB's internal systems, customer information, or confidential materials.

## 1. Fictional Company

**Company:** Horizonte Empreendimentos  
**Industry:** Real estate development  
**Business model:** Residential real estate projects  
**Location:** Campinas, São Paulo  
**Company size:** Medium-sized company (fictional scenario)

## 2. Business Context

Horizonte Empreendimentos manages multiple residential projects and may deal with recurring financial and operational processes, such as:

- Payment collection from customers.
- Monitoring overdue payments.
- Financial reconciliation.
- Communication with customers about outstanding balances.
- Coordination between finance and customer service teams.

These are hypothetical business challenges, not verified facts about a real company.

## 3. ICP Analysis

**Potential ICP fit:** High — hypothetical assessment.

**Reasons:**
- Operates in the real estate development sector.
- May manage recurring customer payments.
- May benefit from more organized collection and reconciliation processes.
- Has a business model that could require integrated financial operations.

**Information still needed:**
- Actual company size and number of projects.
- Current ERP or financial management system.
- Existing payment and collection processes.
- Main operational challenges.
- Decision-makers involved in the purchasing process.
