# AI Sales Intelligence Agent

**AI-powered B2B prospecting and lead intelligence**

## Overview

An AI agent developed during my experience at CUB, designed to support B2B prospecting through company research, lead qualification, and personalized sales preparation.

## The Problem

B2B prospecting required gathering information from multiple sources to understand each company, identify decision-makers, and prepare relevant commercial approaches.

## The Solution

I designed an AI-powered workflow using Manus and Claude to transform company and prospect information into structured commercial intelligence.

## Workflow

1. **Input:** Company name identified in the CRM and provided to the agent.
2. **Company Research:** Research of CNPJ, company history, and relevant business information.
3. **Decision-Maker Identification:** Research of executives and key contacts.
4. **ICP Qualification:** Analysis of the company's alignment with the Ideal Customer Profile.
5. **Business Intelligence:** Identification of potential business challenges and ERP usage, when information was available.
6. **LinkedIn Analysis:** The prospect's LinkedIn profile was provided to the agent to support personalized communication.
7. **Sales Preparation:** Generation of relevant insights and suggested outreach approaches for human review.

The identified business challenges were treated as prospecting hypotheses, not confirmed facts about each company.

## Tools

- CRM as the source of company and prospect information (no direct CRM integration)
