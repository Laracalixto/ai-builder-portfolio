# AI Knowledge Assistant — Internal Support

## Project Overview

An AI-powered internal knowledge assistant designed to help team members access product and process information by consulting recorded product demonstrations and internal training materials.

Unlike a customer-facing chatbot, this agent was developed for internal use, supporting employees in finding information and answering work-related questions.

## Business Context

Developed during my experience at CUB, where product information and internal knowledge were available across recorded product demonstration meetings and training sessions.

## Objective

Make internal knowledge easier to access, helping team members retrieve relevant information and answer questions without having to manually review entire recordings.

## Knowledge Sources

- Recorded product demonstration meetings.
- Internal training recordings.
- Product-related information shared during training sessions.

## Key Capabilities

- Consult information from recorded product demonstrations.
- Access knowledge from internal training materials.
- Answer questions based on the available content.
- Help internal team members find relevant product and process information.

## My Contribution

- Organized and prepared knowledge sources for the agent.
- Configured the agent's instructions and behavior.
- Developed and refined prompts.
- Tested responses using questions related to the available materials.
- Improved the way information could be accessed through the agent.
